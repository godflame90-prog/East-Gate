Privacy Policy

How East Gate collects, uses, shares, and protects your personal information.

East Gate Real-Estate ("East Gate", "we", "us") operates this website and platform, which connects buyers, renters, and land seekers with verified dealers and property professionals. This policy explains what personal data we handle when you use the service, and the choices available to you. It is written to reflect the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and comparable principles.

1. Information we collect

We collect information you give us directly, information created as you use the platform, and a limited set of technical details:

  • Account details — your name, email address, phone number, and password (stored only as a salted scrypt hash, never in plain text).
  • Dealer credentials — for professionals, your business name, CAC number, category, operating state and city, office address, and biography.
  • Listing content — property details, photographs, prices, and documents you submit for review.
  • Enquiries and bookings — the messages you send to dealers, and inspection appointments you request, including dates and any notes you add.
  • Technical data — your IP address (used for security throttling and audit records), browser type, and pages visited.

2. How we use it

  • To create and secure your account, verify your email address, and let you reset your password.
  • To publish approved listings and route enquiries and inspections to the right dealer.
  • To review dealer credentials and listings before they appear publicly.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse, including rate-limiting sign-in and submission attempts.
  • To send transactional email such as verification links, password resets, and booking confirmations.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

3. When we share it

When you enquire about, or book an inspection for, a listing, we share your name, contact details, and message with the dealer who owns that listing so they can respond. We do not sell your personal data. We may share data with service providers who process it on our behalf (for example, email delivery and hosting), and with authorities where the law requires it.

4. Cookies & local storage

We use your browser's local storage to keep you signed in and to remember preferences such as recently viewed properties. This data stays on your device. Clearing it signs you out. We do not use advertising trackers.

5. Retention

We keep account and listing records for as long as your account is active and for a reasonable period afterwards to meet legal and audit requirements. Security records such as audit logs and revocation entries are pruned automatically once they are no longer needed.

6. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, and you may object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

7. Security

Passwords are hashed with scrypt. Sessions are signed and can be revoked, and changing your password signs out every other session. Payments and sensitive documents should never be sent through the public enquiry forms — use the channels an East Gate officer confirms with you.

8. Contact us

Questions about this policy can be sent to privacy@eastgate.ng or through our contact page.

Last updated: 11 October 2026. This policy may change as the service evolves; material changes will be posted on this page.
